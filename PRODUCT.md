# Zahir proto1

Zahir is Paradox Labs' marketing superapp: "the global superapp of Paradox for marketing. You actually use Penrose there, and all of Figaro's work happens there." This prototype puts Zahir inside a minimal Mac browser (a fork of Office Commun's Search, MIT).

## Who it's for
"Everyone": founders with no marketing team, solo marketers, and agencies (the Iron Man suit: it handles the basics, gives you superpowers, or becomes "Iron Man inside your Avengers").

## What it is (Mark's words)
"It's like chatgpt x higgsfield x canva x everything else, but in sexy and easy, initially hidden and intuitively callable toolkit."

- **Figaro** is the digital marketer you hire, "like Jarvis". In Zahir, Figaro is present on every page you open.
- The problem it solves: "one marketer can easily touch around nine different tools", and every switch loses "context, operational knowledge, optimization history". In Zahir, the tools live in one window and Figaro carries the context across them.

## Principles
- **Hidden until called.** The surface is quiet, like the Office Commun site. Tools appear as floating cards when you ask or point, and leave when done.
- **Answers are things, not paragraphs:** ad concepts, images, video frames, layouts, posts, each as a card you can act on.
- **Page-aware.** Figaro offers intents based on the page that's open (a competitor site, Instagram, Gmail).
- Sexy and easy: the finish is the product.

## Scope of proto1
UI only, with fake data. No Penrose or Figaro backend yet ("first we solve UI").

## Visual references (Mark, 2026-09-23)
Office Commun's site (quiet centred text, tilted floating preview cards on hover, black pill button) and Norma's site (inset rounded hero, frosted pill nav, numbered circle-badge cards, grey chips, floating corner pill), both seen inside this browser.

Assumption, inferred and not stated: English UI, and a light theme first because the references are light. Dark mode follows the existing palette pairs.
